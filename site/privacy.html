Privacy policy

Applies to every version of BetterTabs.

The short version

BetterTabs does not collect, store, share or transmit personal data. The extension has no account, no server and no analytics.

What it reads

To draw your tab list, BetterTabs reads the title, web address, window, group and state (pinned, playing) of your open tabs, and the names and colours of your tab groups. These stay in your browser. They are used only to show the list, and are never sent anywhere. The titles and addresses of your open tabs are also kept on your computer as recent tabs (see below).

When you type in the search box, BetterTabs also asks Chrome's history for pages that match, so you can find a page whose tab you closed. It shows up to 30 of them under "From your history" and opens one in a new tab when you click it. It does not copy, store or send your history: the matches are used to draw the list and then dropped.

It has no host permissions and no content scripts, so it cannot read or change the pages themselves.

What it saves

  • The view you chose, the folders you collapsed and your settings, kept on your computer with Chrome's extension storage.
  • Names you give tabs, so they come back when the same page is open after a restart. They are filed under a short hash of the page address, not the address itself.
  • The pages you add to favourites: the title and web address of each, so the icon stays after you close the tab and a click can open the page again. They stay on your computer until you remove them.
  • The lists you save: a name, and the title and web address of each tab you put in it. They stay on your computer until you delete the list.
  • Recent tabs, kept automatically: copies of the titles and web addresses of your open tabs, a new copy about every five minutes while they change, for around half a day, so you can reopen them after a crash. They stay on your computer in Chrome's extension storage, are not synced, and are deleted when you choose Delete recent tabs.

What it sends

Nothing. BetterTabs makes no network requests and loads no remote code. Site icons come from Chrome's own favicon cache.

Add to longbook

On a billing or price page, BetterTabs can open the longbook app on your Mac with the site's name. That link goes to your own computer, not over the internet. If longbook is not installed, BetterTabs shows a link to longbook.app, which opens only if you click it. A setting turns the button off.

This website

The extension sends nothing anywhere. This website, bettertabs.fyi, is different: it uses PostHog to see how visitors find and use it.

What it records. Each page you open, your clicks and how far you scroll, the website you came from and any campaign tag on the link, your browser, operating system, screen size and an approximate location from your IP address, and a screen recording of your visit: how the page looks and where you click and scroll. What you type into a form field is never recorded. It never sees your tabs, and nothing from the extension reaches it.

Cookies. PostHog sets cookies and stores a small amount of data in this browser to tell your visits apart and join them into sessions. They carry a random code, not your name or email.

Who receives it. PostHog (United States) processes the data for me and uses it for nothing else. It is used only to improve this website and the extension's listing.

When it starts. If you are in the European Union, the rest of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or your location cannot be told, a banner asks first and nothing is recorded unless you accept. Elsewhere it starts when the page opens, and you can stop it below. Nothing is recorded if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.
