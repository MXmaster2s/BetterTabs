Privacy policy

Applies to every version of BetterTabs.

The short version

BetterTabs does not collect, store, share or transmit personal data. There is no account, no server and no analytics.

What it reads

To draw your tab list, BetterTabs reads the title, web address, window, group and state (pinned, playing) of your open tabs, and the names and colours of your tab groups. These stay in your browser. They are used only to show the list, and are never sent anywhere. They are not written to disk either, unless you turn on automatic backups (see below).

It has no host permissions and no content scripts, so it cannot read or change the pages themselves.

What it saves

  • The view you chose, the folders you collapsed and your settings, kept on your computer with Chrome's extension storage.
  • Names you give tabs, so they come back when the same page is open after a restart. They are filed under a short hash of the page address, not the address itself.
  • The pages you add to favourites: the title and web address of each, so the icon stays after you close the tab and a click can open the page again. They stay on your computer until you remove them.
  • Only if you turn on automatic backups, which are off until you do: copies of the titles and web addresses of your open tabs, a new copy about every five minutes while they change, for around half a day. They stay on your computer in Chrome's extension storage, are not synced, and are deleted when you turn the setting off or choose Delete all backups.

What it sends

Nothing. BetterTabs makes no network requests and loads no remote code. Site icons come from Chrome's own favicon cache.

Add to longbook

On a billing or price page, BetterTabs can open the longbook app on your Mac with the site's name. That link goes to your own computer, not over the internet. If longbook is not installed, BetterTabs shows a link to longbook.app, which opens only if you click it. A setting turns the button off.

This website

This site is static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
